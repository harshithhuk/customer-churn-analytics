Customer Churn & Sales Analytics

Data-driven insights for revenue growth and customer retention

SQL Power BI Python Data Analysis Business Requirements

Project Overview

🎯 Business Objective

Reduce customer churn by 15% and increase revenue growth through data-driven retention strategies and customer segmentation.

📊 Challenge

Customer churn is increasing and revenue growth is slowing. Needed to identify at-risk customers and regional performance gaps.

💡 Solution

Comprehensive analysis of customer behavior, revenue trends, and churn patterns to inform targeted retention campaigns.

Key Findings & Insights

✅ Regional Performance

  • London leads with highest customer lifetime value (£15,500+)
  • Manchester shows competitive performance with strong customer base
  • Regional churn varies from 20-30% - opportunity for improvement

🎯 Churn Risk Segmentation

  • 5 high-risk customers identified for immediate retention campaigns
  • Average churn customer lifetime value: £1,300 (vs. £4,200 for active customers)
  • Risk increases after 60 days of inactivity

💰 Revenue Drivers

  • Electronics category generates 85% of revenue
  • Laptop Pro (£1,200) is primary revenue driver
  • Product bundling opportunity with accessories (15% of sales)

📈 Customer Segmentation

  • 3 VIP customers (£5,000+ lifetime value) - priority retention
  • 5 Premium customers (£3,000-5,000) - growth opportunity
  • 7 Standard customers (£1,500-3,000) - upsell potential
  • 5 At-Risk customers (<£1,500) - churn prevention focus
